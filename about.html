Our Journey

Founded with a vision to revolutionize the digital landscape, Az Meer(SMC-Private) Limited started as a small team of passionate developers. Today, we stand as a leading software house known for precision, innovation, and unwavering commitment to client success.

2020: The Spark

It all began with a simple idea: to build technology that solves real-world problems. We focused on local startups, helping them build their first digital footprints with robust web foundations.

2022: Expansion

We expanded our expertise into mobile application development and cloud architecture, scaling our operations to serve international clients across Europe, the USA, and the UAE.

2024: Excellence

Now, as a registered SMC-Private Limited company, we continue to lead with advanced AI integration, machine learning solutions, and data-driven marketing strategies for global enterprises.
